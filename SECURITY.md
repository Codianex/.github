# Security Policy

At Codianex we take the security of our software and of our clients' data seriously. Thank you for helping us keep it safe.

## Reporting a vulnerability

**Please do not open a public issue for security problems.**

Email **hello@codianex.com** with the subject **`Security`** and include:

- a description of the issue and its potential impact;
- steps to reproduce (URLs, requests, proof of concept);
- the affected repository, product or domain.

## What to expect

- **Acknowledgement** within **72 hours**.
- An initial assessment and a rough remediation timeline within **7 days**.
- We will keep you informed until the issue is resolved and, if you wish, credit you once it is fixed.

## Scope and good faith

We ask that you give us reasonable time to fix the issue before disclosing it, avoid accessing or modifying data that is not yours, and avoid actions that degrade the service (no denial-of-service, spam or social engineering of our team or clients). We will not pursue legal action against research carried out in good faith under these terms.

### Política de seguridad (ES)

**No abras un issue público para problemas de seguridad.** Escribe a **hello@codianex.com** con el asunto **`Security`** describiendo el problema, cómo reproducirlo y qué afecta. Confirmamos la recepción en **72 horas**, damos una primera evaluación y un plazo estimado en **7 días**, y te mantenemos informado hasta resolverlo. Pedimos divulgación responsable: danos un tiempo razonable para corregirlo y no accedas ni modifiques datos que no sean tuyos.
